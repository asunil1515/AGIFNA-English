About the Conference

The Reserve Conference is a four-day gathering rooted in Jesus' teaching in Matthew 25:1–13 — the parable of the ten virgins.

All ten had lamps. All ten were waiting. But only five carried reserve oil. The heart of this conference is a call to preserve and cultivate the oil: the anointing, intimacy, and ongoing relationship with the Holy Spirit, which cannot be borrowed or manufactured in moments of need.

This conference is not about hype, emotionalism, or momentary encounters alone. It is about forming believers who walk with sustained oil.

FAQs
What exactly is AGIFNA?

AGIFNA is the Assemblies of God India Fellowship of North America. The AGIFNA conference is an annual gathering of believers that have affiliation through membership or association with Indian Assemblies of God churches throughout the US and Canada.

How do I register for the conference?

Registration is completed online through the official conference portal. After registering, you will receive a confirmation email with event details.

Where is the conference located?

The conference will be held at the Houston Marriott Westchase, 2900 Briarpark Dr, Houston, TX 77042. Full venue information will be sent upon registration.

Will transportation be provided from the airport?

Transportation services will be available between both Houston airports and the hotel throughout the weekend. If you need airport transportation or have any travel-related questions, please contact our transportation team: 832-257-6783 | 831-737-6563
